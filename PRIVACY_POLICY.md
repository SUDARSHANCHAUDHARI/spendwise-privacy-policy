# SpendWise Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 21, 2026
_Last updated: March 21, 2026_
**Version:** 1.0.0

SpendWise ("we," "our," or "us") is a personal expense tracking app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Information We Collect

### Financial Data (Stored Locally)
- Transaction records (amounts, categories, notes, dates), budgets, and receipt photos are stored exclusively on your device in an AES-256 encrypted database.
- No account or sign-in is required.

### Camera / Receipt OCR
- When you scan a receipt, the image is processed locally on your device using OCR.
- Receipt images are not transmitted to any server.

## How We Use Your Information

- Store and display your expenses, budgets, and spending charts.
- Process receipt images locally via OCR to pre-fill transaction fields.

We do not transmit your financial data to any SudarshanTechLabs server. We do not sell or share your data.

## Storage and Retention

- All financial data is stored in an AES-256 encrypted database on your device.
- Receipt photos are stored in app-private storage.
- Data is retained until you delete records or uninstall the app.

## Data Security

- Local database is encrypted using AES-256.
- Android's built-in storage encryption provides an additional layer of protection.
- No financial data is ever transmitted over the network.

## Permissions Used

- **CAMERA**: Required to scan receipts for OCR processing.
- **READ_MEDIA_IMAGES**: Required to attach existing photos to expense records.
- **BIOMETRIC**: Used optionally to lock the app with fingerprint or face authentication.

## Your Rights and Controls

- Delete any transaction or receipt at any time within the app.
- Export your transaction history as CSV at any time.
- Enable or disable biometric lock in app settings.

## Children's Privacy

SpendWise is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
